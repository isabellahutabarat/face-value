Face Value

What happens when faces become data? Using candid self-portraits taken while I work, I treat myself as a dataset, and run each image through different systems of classification: emotion labels, valence and arousal dimensions of the circumplex model, and what each image is portrayed as. The same face is sorted again and again, by what it visibly shows, what it seems to feel, and what it might be thinking.

As both the subject and the one doing the classifying, I consider the distance between what a face displays, what others or a model perceives, and what a person actually experiences. How much can an expression tell us, and what is lost when it becomes information?
